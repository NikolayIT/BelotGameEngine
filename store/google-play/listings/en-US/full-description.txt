Play Bulgarian Belot with a computer partner against two computer opponents. Choose the difficulty for each seat and play without an internet connection, registration or ads.

MAKE THE TABLE YOUR OWN
• Five difficulty levels, from random moves to Master.
• Separate difficulty settings for your partner and each opponent.
• Optional custom names for every player.
• Three game speeds, with settings for assists and vibration.

PLAY AND LEARN
• Suit contracts, no trumps and all trumps, with double and redouble.
• Declare combinations and let the game count the points.
• Hints for bidding and card play.
• Gentle dimming of illegal cards to show which moves are allowed.
• Every card you play waits for a tap, including your only legal card and your last card.
• Review the last trick and read the built-in rules.

FOLLOW YOUR PROGRESS
See your rating, wins, losses and completed match history. These statistics track your games against the computer and are saved in the app.

BULGARIAN AND ENGLISH
Switch languages from the home screen or settings.

PLAY WITH PEOPLE
The online-play button opens the separate ednaigra.com website in your system browser. The website needs an internet connection; online multiplayer is not built into the app.

No ads or in-app purchases. Offline games against the computer require no account.
